How it works & safety

Your Jooki fetches the firmware straight from this project's GitHub release, checks it byte-for-byte (sha256), and installs it to the spare half of its storage. The bootloader only switches over if the new system is intact, and rolls back on its own if it fails to start. Nothing overwrites your running system until the copy is verified. Your music, playlists and Wi-Fi live on a separate area and are kept.

The buttons above just open http://<your-jooki>/ll?… links — the Jooki's own local control endpoint on your Wi-Fi. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
