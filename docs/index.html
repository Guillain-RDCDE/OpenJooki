How do I find my Jooki's IP?
  • Easiest — the Jooki app: open it, pick your Jooki, go to its Device / Settings screen; the IP is shown there.
  • Your Wi-Fi router: open its page (often 192.168.1.1) → Connected devices → look for a name starting with jooki.
  • It looks like 192.168.1.42 — four numbers.

A web page isn't allowed to scan your network (your phone blocks that for security), so it needs the address once — then it's saved.

Install OpenJooki (one tap)
Your Jooki downloads OpenJooki, installs it, and restarts on its own (about 10 minutes). Keep it plugged in. It cannot be bricked — it returns to your current firmware if anything fails. Only a Jooki v2 is accepted.
How it works & safety

Your Jooki fetches the firmware straight from this project's GitHub release, checks it byte-for-byte (sha256), and installs it to the spare half of its storage. The bootloader only switches over if the new system is intact, and rolls back on its own if it fails to start. Your music, playlists and Wi-Fi are kept.

The buttons open http://<your-jooki>/… links — the Jooki's own local control on your Wi-Fi. A tiny page that just says ok appears each time: that means the Jooki received the command. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
