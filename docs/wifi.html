Connect the Jooki to Wi-Fi

Moved house, new internet box, new Wi-Fi password? The Jooki only learnt its network from the old app. This page gives it a new one over Bluetooth, from an Android phone or a computer. Nothing to install. (Not from an iPhone: see below.)

This page cannot reach the Jooki from here.

The Jooki listens over Bluetooth when it has no Wi-Fi, and this browser cannot use Bluetooth.

On an iPhone or iPad, no page can: Apple keeps Bluetooth away from every web page, whatever the browser, Chrome included. It is not something a setting can change.

Open this page in Chrome (or Edge) on an Android phone, a Windows PC, a Mac or Linux: two minutes, once. Then the Jooki is back on Wi-Fi, and you use it from your iPhone as usual.

No Chrome at hand? A small program for the computer does the same.

1. Put the Jooki next to this device, switched on.

Its lights may be red or blinking: that is the Jooki looking for Wi-Fi. Bluetooth must be on here.

A list opens: pick JOOKI2_… then Pair (or Connect).

2. Choose the network
Asking the Jooki which networks it can see…

Only 2.4 GHz networks show: the Jooki cannot use 5 GHz. If yours is missing, come closer to the box, or check the box offers a 2.4 GHz network.

Exactly as written on the box or in its app: capitals, spaces and symbols count.

Sending the network to the Jooki…

It takes about ten seconds.

Open my Jooki's page
Networks the Jooki remembers

It joins any of them it can see, by itself. Adding one never removes another. Forget the ones you no longer have.

How it works, and what it cannot do
  • The Jooki's Wi-Fi chip also speaks Bluetooth, and it always offers the same set-up the old app used at the first start. This page speaks it, straight from the browser: nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • The password travels over Bluetooth, over a few metres, without encryption (that is how the Jooki was built). Do it at home.
  • Why not from an iPhone or iPad? Apple does not let any web page use Bluetooth, in any browser (Chrome on an iPhone is Safari underneath). The only way round would be an app on the App Store, which OpenJooki does not have. So this one step needs an Android phone or a computer; everything else works from the iPhone.
  • The Jooki only does 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. If your box only shows a 5 GHz network, turn its 2.4 GHz one on.
  • Once connected, the Jooki's page is at http://jooki.local (or the address your box shows for it), from any phone or computer on that Wi-Fi.
  • Prefer a terminal? jooki_wifi.py does the same on Windows, Mac and Linux.
