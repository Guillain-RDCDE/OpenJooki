# Install OpenJooki (about 15 minutes, from your phone)

For a **Jooki 2** that works, but whose app no longer does.

**You need:** the Jooki switched on and **plugged in**, and a **phone on the same Wi-Fi**.

**Brand new Jooki, never on your Wi-Fi?** Give it your Wi-Fi first, from the
[Wi-Fi page](https://guillain-rdcde.github.io/OpenJooki/wifi.html) (Android phone or computer,
two minutes). No need for the old app, and no need to open the Jooki.

1. **Find your Jooki's address.** Open your internet box's app or page, look at the
   connected devices, and find the one whose name starts with **jooki**. Its address
   looks like `192.168.1.19`.
2. On your phone, open **[guillain-rdcde.github.io/OpenJooki](https://guillain-rdcde.github.io/OpenJooki/)**,
   type that address, tap **Install OpenJooki**, then **Yes, start**.
3. **Wait.** The Jooki installs everything by itself and restarts on its own. Nothing
   happens on screen meanwhile: that's normal. **Don't unplug it.**

Then open its page: type **`http://`** followed by the address, for example
`http://192.168.1.19`. → [Use it](use.md)

**Good to know**
- Your music and tokens are kept.
- If anything goes wrong, switch the Jooki off and on again: it goes back to how it was
  by itself.
- Not sure it's a Jooki 2? No risk: any other model is recognised and nothing happens.
