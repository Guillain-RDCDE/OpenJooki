# The lights, the battery, the charger

## What the lights mean

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| heart white | on |
| heart green | charging (blinking = charging, steady = full) |
| ring off while music plays | a token is playing: normal |
| both side dots white | on the Wi-Fi |
| side dots glowing orange, left then right | looking for the Wi-Fi, for about a minute after switching on |
| **both side dots orange** | no Wi-Fi. Tokens and music still work. See *No Wi-Fi* below |
| side dots light blue | a token was recognised, or a phone opened the page |
| side dots yellow, one pulse | a warning: battery under 20 %, airplane mode on, kids mode switched on or off, or it will switch off in a minute |
| side dots red, two pulses | an error: empty playlist, memory card full, or Spotify could not play |
| heart red, then everything off | switching off (heart held two seconds, or 15 minutes unused on battery, or battery under 10 %) |
| everything very dim | night mode |
| every colour, for 5 seconds | the *Christmas tree* button of the page: just for fun |

## No Wi-Fi (both side dots orange)

- **Away from home**: that's normal. Tokens and music work ([away from home](travel.md)).
- **Moved house, new box, new password**: [give it its Wi-Fi back](https://guillain-rdcde.github.io/OpenJooki/wifi.html).
- **At home, on a Wi-Fi that used to work**: it tries again by itself. On the charger, it
  restarts on its own after ten minutes (twice at most).
- **Still orange after that**: it may be in airplane mode, see just below.

**Side dots stuck orange (or steady red right after start-up) and it won't reconnect?**
It is probably in airplane mode, switched on from the Jooki itself. Switch it off, then
switch it on while **holding the track knob turned to *Next*** (to the right) until the
start-up chime.

## It doesn't start any more

- **Nothing lights up, not even the heart**: the battery is flat. Plug a charger for an
  hour: the Jooki should switch itself on when the cable goes in.
- **The heart lights up, but the side dots never do and there is no chime**: nine times
  out of ten its memory card is worn out. [A new card](https://guillain-rdcde.github.io/OpenJooki/sdcard.html#new)
  brings it back.

## Charging

- Use a phone charger of **1 A at most**, a clean port and a cable in good shape, and keep
  an eye on the Jooki while it charges (Jooki's own advice, May 2024).
- The port is for charging **or** wired headphones, not both at once.
- **Worn battery** (only works plugged in, or switches off soon after): the cell can be
  replaced; it must have its temperature sensor, or it won't charge. Owners explain how:
  [the details](../docs/24-hardware-and-lights.md#3-the-battery).
