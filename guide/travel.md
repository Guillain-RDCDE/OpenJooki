# Away from home

**What your child uses works without Wi-Fi.** The characters, the knob and the buttons play
the music that is already on the Jooki, in the car, at grandma's, in a tent.

| Away from home, without Wi-Fi | |
|---|---|
| Characters with the music you added | work |
| Knob, buttons, volume | work |
| Characters with Spotify, web radios | need the Internet: without it they play nothing |
| The Jooki's page (add songs, settings) | needs the Jooki and your phone on the same Wi-Fi |
| Night mode (low volume, dim lights, sleep timer) | needs the time: see below |

## Before you leave

- **Add the music now.** Away from home you cannot add songs without the page.
- **Charge it.** On battery, it switches itself off after 15 minutes with nothing playing.

## When you get there

- After switching it on, the two side dots glow left and right for about a minute, then
  stay **orange**: it means "no Wi-Fi". That's normal away from home. Characters and music
  already work.
- On the charger, it may restart once or twice by itself, without its chime: it is looking
  for its Wi-Fi. Then it stops trying.

## Night mode away from home

The Jooki has no clock of its own: it takes the time from the Internet, and forgets it when
switched off. Without the Internet it does not know whether it is night, so **night mode
waits**.

It comes back as soon as the Jooki knows the time:

- on a Wi-Fi with the Internet, by itself;
- or **open the Jooki's page once from your phone**: the Jooki takes your phone's time
  (OpenJooki 2.1.4 or later).

After each start without the Internet, open the page again.

## The page away from home

The page works when the Jooki and your phone are on the same Wi-Fi. A Wi-Fi without the
Internet is fine (a holiday home's box, a campsite).

- **A new Wi-Fi** (holiday home): give it to the Jooki with the
  [Wi-Fi page](https://guillain-rdcde.github.io/OpenJooki/wifi.html), from an Android phone
  or a computer. Then open `http://jooki.local` (or the name you gave it).
- **Your phone's own Wi-Fi (hotspot)**: do it **at home, before leaving**. Switch the
  hotspot on; on an iPhone also switch on *Maximize Compatibility* (the Jooki only sees
  2.4 GHz Wi-Fi). Give that Wi-Fi to the Jooki with the Wi-Fi page. Away from home, switch
  the hotspot on, then the Jooki.
- **Back home**, on the Wi-Fi page, **Forget** the Wi-Fi you added. The Jooki works best
  when it knows only the Wi-Fi next to it.
