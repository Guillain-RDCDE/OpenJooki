# Update it (one tap)

When a new version is out, the Jooki's page shows a message. Tap **Update now**.

1. Keep the Jooki **plugged in**.
2. The page shows the steps: downloading, checking, installing, restarting. About ten
   to twenty minutes.
3. The Jooki restarts on its own, and the page comes back with the new version.

No message? Settings → **Check for updates**.

**Good to know**
- Your music and tokens are never touched by an update.
- If the new version does not start, unplug the Jooki and plug it back in: it goes back
  to the previous one by itself.
